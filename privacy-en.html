2. Information processed

PhotoPDF processes images, documents, recognized text, crop coordinates and page settings that you choose to use for scanning, editing, OCR and PDF creation. Android system file pickers and camera intents are used to access user-selected content.

3. On-device OCR

In the reviewed Stage 20 source code, OCR runs on the device using bundled language data. Document images do not need to be uploaded to a developer server for OCR. PDF files are saved to locations selected by the user. If OCR fails, the app may create image-only PDFs without selectable text.

4. Websites and internet access

The web-to-PDF feature uses Android WebView to load websites you choose. Websites may receive your IP address, device/browser information and cookies and may use their own tracking technologies. Third-party sites are governed by their own privacy policies. WebView may retain cookies or site storage through Android's WebView mechanisms.

5. Sharing and third parties

When you share, print or open a document using another app, the content is provided to the app or service you select. System camera, printing and PDF viewing components may also process content under their own terms.

6. Ads, analytics and accounts

The reviewed Stage 20 source does not show embedded advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or account registration. This must be rechecked for the actual release and future versions.

7. Retention and deletion

Original files normally remain in their original locations. Exported PDFs are saved where you choose. Temporary processing files may be stored in app cache and deleted after processing or when cache is cleared. Photos captured through the system camera may remain in device media storage. Uninstalling the app may not delete files saved outside private app storage.

8. Security and children

PhotoPDF relies on Android file-access and app-isolation mechanisms. No software can guarantee perfect security. The app is not specifically directed to children.
